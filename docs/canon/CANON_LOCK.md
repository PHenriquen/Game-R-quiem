# Canon Lock — Réquiem: Ecos do Silêncio

This file exists so technical experimentation cannot quietly rewrite the identity of the game.

## Locked pillars

1. **Réquiem is a finite authored game first.** The campaign, Noah, Vesper, the Silêncio and the emotional arc are the core work. Replay systems extend the game; they do not replace it.
2. **Noah remains a quiet protagonist.** His inner life is communicated through action, hesitation, posture, objects, spaces, memory fragments and consequences rather than constant exposition.
3. **The world tells the story.** Environmental storytelling, recurring symbols, architecture, item placement and optional discoveries must carry information that a player can miss.
4. **Rhythm is mastery, not permission.** A player may act outside the beat. Timing improves execution, score, Cadência and expression; it must not turn normal combat into a binary rhythm lock.
5. **Cards are actions.** The hand changes combat decisions in real time. It is not primarily a stack of passive stat buffs.
6. **The campaign is not generated from beatmaps.** Authored encounters may use the same rhythm infrastructure, but narrative pacing remains manually directed.

## Protagonist — visual identity that must survive iteration

**Noah** is the canonical protagonist name. Earlier working names remain historical only.

- small, unmistakably non-human silhouette against large environments;
- smooth dark face with large ivory eye-shapes that carry expression;
- white stylized hair built as one compact mass, with relaxed downward spikes and two short front fringes;
- short **white mantle** over a black underlayer;
- **crimson scarf** as the strongest warm identifying accent;
- small aged-gold bell at the collar;
- spectral-blue Heart fragment kept visually quiet outside high Cadência;
- narrow spectral blade;
- effects stay controlled until combat earns visual intensity.

The silhouette must work with six anchors: hair mass, eyes, mantle, scarf, bell and blade. Extra symbols, armor plates and surface decoration are rejected unless they replace an anchor rather than join the pile.

## Emotional pillars

Every major feature should support at least one of these:

- solitude while exploring;
- curiosity while discovering what the world refuses to explain directly;
- mastery while combat begins to feel musical;
- guilt, memory and personhood emerging through what Noah learns and chooses.

## First-region anchor

The **Catedral Afogada** remains the first major location direction: monumental, partially submerged, old bells and incomplete sequences, silence used as an active part of the atmosphere.

## Reference rule

References may contribute principles, never final shapes or copied content. Hollow Knight, Hades, Lies of P, Silent Hill 2, rhythm games and action anime are vocabulary sources, not asset or story templates.

## Change policy

A change to a locked pillar needs a deliberate entry in `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` explaining:

- what changes;
- why the existing rule failed;
- what is preserved;
- what player experience improves.

If a technical feature conflicts with this document, change the feature first.
